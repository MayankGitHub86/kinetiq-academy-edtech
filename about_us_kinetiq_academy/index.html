The Problem We Solve

Passive consumption builds an illusion of competence.

When engineers watch pre-recorded walkthroughs, the presenter does all the cognitive heavy lifting. In production, bugs don't present clean stack traces, and distributed failures never follow a linear script.

We replace passive observation with hostile sandboxes: we drop simulated network partitions, unhandled race conditions, and corrupted WAL logs directly into your terminal. You only graduate once your mental model survives production chaos.

Our Core Operating Values

How we build our company, calibrate our software, and design educational benchmarks.

Rigor over Hype

> No hand-waving abstractions
02/

Build for Longevity

Systems and cognitive structures should withstand a decade of evolution. We engineer mental models that remain valid across language shifts, runtime updates, and hardware generations.
