Can I cancel or alter my subscription at any time? expand_more

Yes. You can cancel, downgrade, or upgrade your subscription from your account dashboard with a single click. If you cancel, your full platform privileges will remain active until the end of your prepaid billing period, with zero cancellation penalties.

How do the interactive cloud sandboxes work? expand_more

Every sandbox spins up a dedicated MicroVM in under 800ms directly in your browser. Unlike static WebAssembly playgrounds, these are actual isolated Linux kernels with root privileges, root file-systems, network routing tables, and real observability tooling like eBPF and Wireshark.
