Variables declared with let and const are hoisted to the top of their block scope but remain uninitialized in the Temporal Dead Zone (TDZ) until execution reaches the line where they are declared.

Unlike var (which binds to undefined upon creation phase), referencing an identifier during TDZ strictly triggers a runtime ReferenceError to prevent premature read leaks.
